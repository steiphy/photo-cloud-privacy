In short

Photo Cloud works entirely on your phone. It does not upload your photos, does not collect personal data, has no account, no ads and no analytics.

What the app accesses

  • Your photo library, only after you allow it. The app reads photos to show them, make small preview images, sort them into albums, find duplicates and let you search them.
  • Sorting uses the image-recognition built into your phone (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android). It runs on the device; photos are not sent to any server.

What the app stores

Preview images, album assignments and search tags are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Deleting photos

Photo Cloud deletes a photo only when you choose Delete for a duplicate and confirm in your phone's system dialog. On iPhone, deleted photos go to Recently Deleted.

Sharing with third parties

None. The app makes no network requests with your data and contains no third-party tracking or advertising code.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: contact the developer through the app's store page.
